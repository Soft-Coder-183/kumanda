# Kumanda 1.0.0 (2026-11-13)

The first release.

- Pick any project on your computer and write prompts to Claude Code from your phone or another computer; the reply streams live.
- Continue chats you started in the terminal from where you left off.
- When Claude asks for permission or asks a question you see it as a card: Allow, Always allow, Deny. You can change the permission mode and the model from the chat.
- If your connection drops the work goes on on the computer; when you come back you see it from where it was.
- All your projects' products in one list: HTML pages and videos open directly, an APK installs on the phone, EXE/ZIP download. Copies and old versions are kept apart.
- You can attach a photo or a screenshot to your prompt.
- If you want, your phone gets a notification when Claude finishes or waits for permission.
- Protected by a single password; you can see and remove logged-in devices in Settings.
