# Kumanda 2.3.0

## New
- **Skills and commands with `/`.** Type `/` at the start of a prompt to see the project's skills, your own commands and Claude
  Code's built-in commands with their descriptions. Keep typing to filter; ↑/↓ choose, Enter or Tab complete, Esc closes.
  Commands that only work in the terminal are not offered.
- **Claude artifacts.** When Claude publishes a page to claude.ai, the chat shows a card with an "Open on claude.ai" link. The
  chat's Products panel and the project's Products tab list the artifacts too.

## Fixed
- **Project order in the sidebar.** Projects are sorted by the time of their last message, not by when their files last changed.
  Claude Code sometimes rewrites old chat files, which made old projects without real chats jump to the top.
